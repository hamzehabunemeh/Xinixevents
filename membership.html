The idea

Not everyone is a member

XINIX Membership recognises the people who are part of the XINIX story: the ones we want in the room. It is a status earned through relationship and presence, never bought.

By invitation only

Every member is chosen personally and receives a private, named invitation. There is no public application and no sign-up form.

Never for sale

Membership cannot be purchased, sold or transferred. It is a recognition, not a product.

No annual fee

There is no membership fee and no automatic paid renewal. Membership stays active through your place in the community.

The tiers

Three levels. One circle.

Each tier is a physical metal card, engraved and verified. Tap a card to see its reverse.

Member

Personal invitation from XINIX

A recognised member of the XINIX community. Brushed silver metal with dark engraving.

Insider

Personal invitation or management selection

An inner-circle member with selected access privileges. Matte black metal with silver engraving.

Founder

Personal selection · maximum 50 people

The Founding 50 identity, with VIP access at all XINIX events. Black metal with gold engraving, numbered 001/050 to 050/050.

Card artwork shown is illustrative. The QR on each card is unique to its holder.

50
Founding 50

Fifty cards. Ever.

The Founder edition is capped at 50 physical cards, numbered 001/050 to 050/050. Each recipient is selected personally by XINIX management.

  • Not for sale. Founder membership cannot be bought.
  • Non-transferable. It belongs to the person it was issued to.
  • VIP access at all XINIX events, subject to venue capacity, safety requirements, event configuration and coordination with the venue.
  • Some numbers are reserved for individuals personally chosen by the owner and management. The rest are allocated gradually.

Founder membership is a founding recognition. It is not equity, ownership, a partnership or a financial interest in XINIX. VIP access does not automatically guarantee a guest, a table, a specific seat, alcohol, or entry after capacity is reached; any guest entitlement is stated separately for the relevant event. Event privileges remain subject to the same safety and conduct requirements for every member.

How it works

From invitation to card

  1. 01

    Selection

    XINIX management identifies a suitable guest and chooses their tier.

  2. 02

    Private invitation

    A named, personal invitation is sent. Membership is never openly advertised.

  3. 03

    Acceptance

    The invitee accepts, and we collect only the details we genuinely need.

  4. 04

    The card

    A numbered metal card is engraved with the member's name, tier and number.

  5. 05

    Activation

    The membership is activated only once the card is issued and tested.

  6. 06

    Recognition

    The member receives their confirmation and a clear explanation of their privileges.

The reverse carries a unique QR that is checked live against the official XINIX record at the door. It contains no personal details, and a screenshot or a photo of a card is never accepted as proof of membership. Lost or copied? We revoke the old card and issue a new one.

Staying active

Fair by design

Membership works on an active-status model. It is a living circle, and it is handled with respect.

Reviewed periodically

Management reviews membership activity from time to time, around once a year.

One missed event is fine

Missing a single event is never, on its own, a reason to lose your place.

We talk first

Before any change of status we contact the member and, where reasonably possible, allow 30 days to respond.
