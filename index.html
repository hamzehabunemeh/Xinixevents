About

A night is an experience

XINIX Events is an event production and entertainment company built on one idea: a night out should feel like something you were part of, not just somewhere you went.

We curate electronic music nights, book the DJs and artists who define the sound, and design every detail of the room around them, from stage and lighting to crowd flow, safety and guest care.

Whether it's a ticketed public night or an invitation-only gathering for a select guest list, we plan with the same discipline: clear communication, polished production and an atmosphere people remember long after the last track.
